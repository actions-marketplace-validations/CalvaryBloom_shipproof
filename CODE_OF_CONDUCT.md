# Code of Conduct

ShipProof is an open project for people of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

To report conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly as practical.
